How the forecast works

The calculator runs 40,000 simulated breedings with the same rules the mod uses, then groups foals by size class and by how good their speed, jump and health genes are. The three biggest groups are shown.

  • Guidebook ratings are ranges. Each line in the book adds up 4 hidden genes with 2 copies each, scored 0 to 8: Abysmal 0, Poor 1–2, Average 3–5, Good 6–7, Excellent 8. Every gene combination that fits the rating is considered.
  • Each parent passes one copy of every gene, picked at random, exactly as in the mod. That's why a foal can beat both parents.
  • Speed and jump add Athleticism and Energy to their own three lines. Speed in blocks per second is the speed attribute × 42.16. Jump height uses Minecraft's jump-strength curve. Vanilla horses run 4.7–14.2 blocks/s and jump 1.1–5.3 blocks.
  • Max health is 15 + half of (lung + gut + hoof + immune), then scaled by adult size, so big horses get more health. Gray, silver dapple and leopard can each take a little away.
  • Height. LCORL Large adds 3% per copy. HMGA2 Small takes off 6% with one copy and 19% with two. About 20 hidden genes add the rest, so a parent's height (from its inventory screen) is used to estimate them. A foal's adult height is also pulled 30% toward its mother's height.
  • Size classes follow the mod: under 317.5 kg is too small to ride, over 635 kg fits two riders. Pony-sized means 14.2 hands or less.
  • Fill from screenshots reads the guidebook with Tesseract.js, a text reader that runs in your browser, so images never leave your device. It only accepts values the mod can produce, and leaves a box alone when a reading is unclear. Full-size screenshots of the open book work best.
  • Assumes the mod's default settings (sizes, genetic stats and health effects all on).
